**Subject:** Copy Advice request — non-broadcast social video, gambling-adjacent
(CAP Code section 16)

Hello,

We'd be grateful for advice on a proposed non-broadcast video ad before we commit to
production.

**About us.** Tiki Acca (tikiacca.com) is a free app where groups of friends each pick
one leg of a shared football accumulator. We are not a bookmaker: we take no stakes and
place no bets. Members score points on a group leaderboard, and can optionally place
the finished acca themselves with a licensed UK bookmaker. We expect to carry
bookmaker affiliate links, so we are treating this ad as gambling marketing under
section 16.

**The ad.** 50 seconds (with 30s, 15s and 6s cutdowns) for paid social (Meta, possibly
TikTok), age-gated 18+. Footage is AI-generated and will be labelled as such. Set in a
floodlit 5-a-side cage at night, watched from a neutral touchline. Each pass is
intercut with a real screen from our app showing that player's pick landing on the
group's betslip.

- **Act 1:** four friends make their picks. Three are sensible; the fourth (Kev)
  takes by far the longest price, ignores an unmarked teammate, and skies his shot over
  the fence. His leg loses, the group scores −1 point, and his friends tease him —
  hands on heads, laughter, a slow clap. He apologises sheepishly.
- **Act 2:** a second group's picks all win. They score a goal and celebrate the goal.
  The ad ends on a points leaderboard nobody on screen reacts to, then an end card:
  *"Your mates. One acca. Every leg counts."* and *"We're not a bookmaker. You bet with
  licensed UK bookmakers — we keep the score. 18+. BeGambleAware.org"*.
- **Hook VO:** *"Tiki-taka is football's great passing game: everyone touches the
  ball. In a group acca, everyone touches the bet."*

**What we've done to comply.** All characters are written and cast at 27–30 and we
will discard any rendering that could seem under 25. No real players or lookalikes,
badges, kits, crests, stadiums or competition branding; club names appear only as
betslip text and exclude the "big six". No money is shown at any point: no stakes,
returns, payouts, balances or currency, only combined odds and points. The app screens
are real captures of the shipping product.

**Our questions:**

1. **16.3.7 (peer pressure).** The teasing is aimed at one friend's *choice of pick*,
   never at anyone for not betting. Is teasing a bad pick within the rule, or could it
   be read as social pressure around betting?
2. **16.3.7 (the hook line).** Does *"in a group acca, everyone touches the bet"*
   read as a description of how the product works, or as implying everyone in a group
   should bet?
3. **16.3.6 (recognition and admiration).** In Act 2 the group celebrates a goal, not
   their picks, and the leaderboard is shown without reaction. Is that sufficient
   separation, or should we cut the leaderboard?
4. **Points as reward.** Is showing group points and a leaderboard (no money) as the
   payoff acceptable, or could points themselves be seen as linking gambling to
   status?
5. **Trust line.** Is the end card trust line, shown for at least 3 seconds in every
   cut, sufficient in placement and wording?

We can supply the storyboard, full script and sample app screens. Thank you.
