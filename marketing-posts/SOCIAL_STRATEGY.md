## Goal

Take @TikiAcca from **0 → a healthy, engaged following**. "Healthy" here means an
audience that replies, screenshots, and invites their own group — not a vanity count.

## Positioning (one line)

We don't compete with bookmakers or tipsters. We own a moment nobody else sells:
**"whose leg lost the acca?"** Every post ladders up to **accountability + banter +
receipts** — the social contract of the shared acca.

- Lead with **Territory A** — *Every leg counts* (accountability).
- Prove with **Territory B** — *Banter, with receipts* (the scoreboard).
- Flavour with **Territory C** — *The Saturday ritual* (togetherness).

## Voice guardrails (non-negotiable — from the brief)

**Always:** speak to the group ("your group"), name the ritual (Saturday, the group
chat, the pub), keep odds and points precise, product-as-hero (real UI screenshots).

**Never:** "guaranteed", "risk-free", "easy money", "smart bets", win-rate promises,
or anything a tipster would say. No urgency mechanics ("odds boost ends soon!").

**Compliance:** 18+ and GambleAware live in the **bio + pinned post**. Add
`Not a bookmaker · 18+` (and GambleAware for anything paid) to any post that
explicitly mentions odds or betting. We keep score; we never hold money or place bets.

## Launch learnings (July 2026 — first post)

First post was a **6-tweet welcome thread** (product tour: hero → leaderboard →
receipts → best odds → stats → CTA). Result: **~110 views, 0 likes.**

**Diagnosis — this was distribution, not copy:**

1. **0 followers = ~0 organic reach.** X samples a post to your followers first; with
   none, there's no early-engagement signal, so reach never expands. ~110 views is the
   floor for any brand-new account. Rewriting copy does not beat this from the timeline.
2. **A long thread is the worst cold-start format.** Threads reward accounts with an
   existing audience reading top-to-bottom; self-reply threads get *less* initial
   distribution, and tweets 2–6 (our best assets) were seen by almost nobody.
3. **Announcement, not conversation.** "Sign up / tag your squad" asks strangers who
   don't trust us yet to do work → no reason to reply → no engagement → no reach.
4. **We broadcast into a void.** Launches work by showing up where the football
   conversation already is, not by posting into an empty timeline.

**What changes (do this, not more posting):**

- **80% of X time goes to replies, not posts** — especially on matchdays. A good reply
  on a big football post reaches more relevant people than any post on our own timeline.
  The profile (bio + pinned) does the converting. See **Reply playbook** below.
- **Break the thread into 6 singles**, one per day. Each tweet is a strong standalone.
- **First-mover posts are questions** (the "One Leg Each" format), not announcements.
- **Pin a single tweet** (hero + one-line pitch), never a thread.
- **Seed the first hour manually** — get 5–10 real people (our own groups, mates) to
  reply/quote in the first hour so X sees an engagement signal. Real network, not bots.

**Reset the scoreboard:** at 0 followers, weekly success is *replies from football
accounts, replies to "One Leg Each", and profile→site clicks* — **not likes**. Cold-start
growth is measured in weeks of consistent replying, not one launch post. A quiet launch
thread is normal and is **not** a signal to change the product or brand.

## Reply playbook (the actual growth engine)

Replies — not our own timeline — are how a 0-follower account gets discovered. Reply to
football fan/acca/bet-builder accounts with something genuinely funny or useful.
**Rules:** no links in cold replies (X suppresses them and it reads as spam); add value
or banter first; only mention Tiki Acca when it's genuinely relevant; stay inside the
voice guardrails (never tipster language); the profile does the selling. 18+ context only.

| Situation you're replying to | Reply template |
|------------------------------|----------------|
| Someone posts their acca / betslip | "Solid card. Genuine question — did the group agree on this or did one person just build it and drop it in the chat? 👀" |
| "Who's on the bet builder today?" | "One leg each is the only fair way to do it. Nobody gets to blame the guy who picked all five when it dies." |
| A big acca lands / someone's buzzing | "Every mate in that group chat currently claiming *their* leg was the banker 😂" |
| A one-leg-lets-down-the-acca post | "The 'whose leg killed it' inquest is the best part of the weekend and you can't tell me otherwise." |
| "Group chat is chaos / spreadsheet died" | "The abandoned-since-November acca spreadsheet is a universal experience 💀" |
| Someone brags they're the group's best tipster | "Bold claim. Does the group actually keep score or are we going off vibes? Because vibes lie." |
| Debate about whose picks are better | "This is exactly the argument a leaderboard was invented to end." |
| Matchday "who you got" post | "Give me one leg each off this timeline and we've got an acca. I'll start: [pick] @ [odds]." |
| Someone's leg dies first | "First one out. He's not living this down till Christmas." |
| "Football's better with mates" sentiment | "This. The bet's just the excuse — the group chat is the actual game." |

**Cadence for replies:** aim for 5–10 quality replies/day, concentrated around kickoffs
Fri–Sun. Quote-tweet (with a take) sparingly when a post is a perfect fit for the
product — that earns reach without reading as an ad. Track which reply styles get
profile clicks and lean into those.

## The cold-start plan

Zero followers means content must be shareable and reply-baiting *on its own merits* —
we have no audience to distribute it yet. So:

1. **Reply more than you post**, especially Saturdays. A no-follower account gets
   discovered in the replies of football accounts, not from its own timeline.
2. **Every post is screenshot-first.** The real UI is the best asset — never
   illustrate what can be screenshotted.
3. **Run one repeatable, anticipated format:** **"One Leg Each"** every Friday — post
   a fixture card, ask the timeline to build a 5-leg acca in the replies. Free
   content, community, and a live demo of the core mechanic.
4. **Seed with real groups.** Get a handful running; screenshot their (anonymised)
   leaderboards. Real receipts beat mock data.

## Content pillars (rough mix)

1. **The Moment (30%)** — shared sweat / whose-leg-lost-it. The differentiator. Highest shareability.
2. **Product clarity (25%)** — the 8 graphics; each teaches one thing.
3. **Football banter / reply-bait (25%)** — polls, "one leg each, who you got?". Our follower-acquisition engine.
4. **Proof / receipts (10%)** — real settled results, leaderboards.
5. **Trust / RG (10%)** — "not a bookmaker, we keep score" — doubles as differentiation.
